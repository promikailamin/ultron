Chiseled packed ice is a decorative chiseled variant of packed ice.


== Obtaining ==


=== Breaking ===
Chiseled packed ice drops as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, it drops nothing.


=== Crafting ===


=== Stonecutting ===


== Usage ==
Chiseled packed ice is a purely decorative block used for building.
It does not melt when exposed to high light levels and does not create water when broken.
Unlike regular packed ice, chiseled packed ice uses the default friction value and does not cause players and entities on top of it to slide.
