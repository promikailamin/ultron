A blank disc is a craftable item that can be turned into track discs with custom music tracks in a disc press.


== Obtaining ==


=== Crafting ===


== Usage ==
When a player uses a blank disc on a jukebox, a message will be displayed on the action bar: "Use a Disc Press to make this Blank Disc playable."


=== Disc press ===
A blank disc can be added in a disc press to create a track disc with a custom music track.
