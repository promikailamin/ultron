Pizza is a food item that has been referenced multiple times early on in Minecraft's development. On November 15, 2013, Nathan Adams joked that pizza would be a good idea to add while teasing something else. In response, Markus Persson tweeted that he liked that idea. Kristoffer Jelbring jokingly responded that pizza is confirmed to be added. Nathan Adams followed up by jokingly suggesting that Persson and Jelbring will add it. In addition, the item has appeared in multiple add-ons. Pizza can refer to the following topics.
