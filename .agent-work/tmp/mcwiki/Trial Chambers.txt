Trial chambers are uncommon underground structures that feature trial spawners and vaults. They consist mostly of copper blocks and tuff bricks, and are the only place where breezes spawn, whose breeze rods can be used to craft wind charges. The vaults found here are unlocked using trial keys, which are commonly obtained from clearing trial spawners.
Drinking an ominous bottle inside a trial chamber triggers an ominous trial, an event where trial spawners become ominous trial spawners. Ominous trial spawners summon mobs wearing high-power gear, and clearing an ominous trial spawner has a chance of giving an ominous key. Ominous keys can be used on ominous vaults, which give greater rewards including a low chance of a heavy core, which is used to craft a mace.


== Generation ==
Trial chambers generate underground in the Overworld. The starting room generates at an altitude of between Y=-40 and -20, but the structure is not restricted to those Y levels; the majority of the structure's rooms tend to generate between Y=-20 and 0.[verify]
The generation of trial chambers follows a grid of 34×34 chunk regions centered on the world origin (X=0, Z=0). There is always one trial chambers structure generated in each region at a random location, unless a deep dark biome cancels the generation of the trial chambers.
While the origin point of the structure (corridor_end) cannot generate within a deep dark biome, other parts of it can intersect a deep dark biome.
Trial chambers are one of the only structures that generate on the same locations on Java Edition and Bedrock Edition.


=== Locating ===
Trial chambers can be located using buried trial chambers maps, which can be obtained in two ways.

Buried trial chambers maps are sold by journeyman-level cartographer villagers for 12 emeralds and a compass. The maps always point to the nearest trial chamber relative to the cartographer that does not already have a buried trial chambers map that points to it. All maps bought from the same cartographer always point to the same structure.
Buried trial chambers maps can be found in loot of secret chests in abandoned camps. (see Abandoned Camp § Secret chests).
Trial chambers are buried underground, so exposed caves will usually have weird shapes that will determine the location of a trial chamber.
In Java Edition, a given villager always trades the same map, which is determined upon the trade unlocking.


=== Unintended Features ===
Blocks such as glow berries, glow lichen, sculk veins, brown mushrooms and red mushrooms can spawn in various rooms when they are near the corresponding caves.


== Structure ==

The starting room of the structure is corridor/end. This room connects to a chamber and a section of a corridor. At the end of the section of a corridor, an intersection links to another chamber and another section of a corridor is generated, but this time it ends on the atrium, which then leads to the entrance room.
The structure then branches out from some corridor/straight pieces.


=== File structure ===
All trial chambers structures found below are located in the folder client.jar/data/minecraft/structure/trial_chambers‌[JE  only] or data/behavior_packs/vanilla/structures/trial_chambers‌[BE  only].

=== Components ===


==== Corridors ====

Trial chambers are composed of two corridors:

a corridor going from the entrance room to the intersection, passing through four slices and the atrium;
a corridor going from the intersection to the end room, passing through four slices.
The intersection connects both corridors, forming an L-shape. The intersection and the end room always lead to a chamber (see § Chambers), while slices have a probability to lead to a hallway (see § Hallways).


===== Entrance =====

The entrance generates at the end of one of the two corridors, passing through the atrium. Each entrance contains a "doorway" that consists of waxed oxidized copper blocks, including its cut copper and chiseled copper variants.
entrance_1 and entrance_2 include a vault, two entrance chests, and a platform containing either an oak, a mangrove, or a cactus; additionally, entrance_1 has a secret room containing a bed and a chest.
entrance_3 includes two vaults and three entrance chests.


===== Atrium =====

The atrium generates between the entrance and one of the corridor's slices. 


===== Slices =====

Corridor slices, or straight pieces, are sub-structures that make up the corridors; they are generated on top of a tuff brick plate. Each of the two corridors has four slices, composed randomly.
Except for straight_1 and straight_8, all slices have a copper door:

straight_2 and straight_5 generate a copper door on the lower level;
straight_3 and straight_6 generate a copper door on the middle level;
straight_4 and straight_7 generate a copper door on the upper level.
On average, each trial chamber has 6.29 (44⁄7) doors. These copper doors can either lead directly to a chamber, a hallway or nothing. Provided the latter case doesn't happen:

lower level doors always lead to a descending spiral staircase (1⁄2 chance between lower_hallway_connector or trapped_staircase);
upper level doors always lead to an ascending spiral staircase (upper_hallway_connector);
middle level doors and these spiral staircases have a 97.48% chance to directly lead to a chamber, 2.44% chance to lead to a hallway piece, and 0.08% chance to directly lead to a dead-end (see § Hallways);
any doors in any level, especially the spiral staircases leading to them in the case of upper and lower level doors, can also generate dead-end pieces as fallback pieces.
Both straight_6 and straight_8 generate with a trial spawner that can either spawn a melee, small melee or ranged mob (see § Mobs), but always never breezes. straight_7 generates with a hidden vault.
Slices can generate with various decorations such as flower pots, decorated pots, candles and corridor barrels (see § Decor). Slices can have addons, some of them generate with a trial spawner (that can either spawn a melee, small melee or ranged mob, but never breezes), a dispenser trap or a vault. Some slices cannot contain specific types of or all corridor addons.


===== End =====

chamber_2

End rooms always include an ominous vault. In addition to its ominous vault, end_2 also features a regular vault and an chest with intersection loot.


==== Intersections ====

The intersection is the room that connects both corridors. It always features a safer zone equipped with beds as well as an entrance to a chamber (rarely a hallway, see § Hallways for more information about the hallway generation rules.). In addition to that:

intersection_1 and intersection_2 feature an ominous vault;
intersection_2 and intersection_3 feature a barrel with loot;
intersection_2 features a secret room with an additional bed and a decorated pot always containing 3 strings.


==== Hallways ====

Hallways are segments that connect corridors to chambers. When a copper door generates in a corridor, it (or, in the case of an upper or lower door, the spiral staircase that follows it; see § Slices) may lead to one of the following pieces:

a chamber (97.48%);
a hallway segment (1.14%);
an encounter (0.41%);‌[Java Edition  only]
a dead end (0.08%).
If the selected piece cannot be generated, the game attempts to generate a different piece. After a certain number of failed attempts, it defaults to generating a dead end.​[more information needed] If the dead end itself cannot be generated, no piece is generated.
This process is then repeated at the end of each hallway segment or encounter. Furthermore, hallways never branch, meaning that every copper door found in corridors eventually leads to a single chamber, a dead end, or nothing.


===== Encounters =====

Encounters are hallway pieces that consist in "short challenges leading to a larger chamber". They contain a single trial spawner and a vault.


==== Chambers ====

Chambers are large combat rooms that contain multiple trial spawners and vaults, as well as one ominous vault. Chambers can be accessed in numerous ways:

they have a chance to generate in corridor slices (chambers can generate straight from slices by middle level copper doors) and hallways, in this case, all chambers can generate;
they can always generate in intersections, also in this case, all chambers can generate;
additionally, assembly, chamber_1, eruption and slanted can generate in the end of corridor ends.
Each chamber design has several potential entrances, of which only one is connected to the rest of the structure. The remaining entrances are sealed with an entrance cap (see § Miscellaneous). Each potential entrance, including the sealed ones, generates a disposal consisting of a hopper placed above a barrel (see § Decor), allowing players to manage their inventory before entering a chamber.

== Mobs ==

Natural mob spawning do not occur within the trial chambers. Instead, mobs can spawn in the trial chambers through trial spawners or unnatural mob spawning sources.

Mobs from trial spawners

These mobs above spawn from the trial spawners. The number of mobs spawned scales with the number of players in range of each spawner, and breezes are spawned less per spawner and per player than other mobs.
When a trial chamber generates, one mob from each of the following categories is randomly selected for the trial spawners in that trial chamber. Every mob within the same category has an equal chance to be selected. There are four (or five if the slow ranged category is counted) categories for trial chamber mob spawning.

Breeze (always spawn a breeze)
Melee (1⁄3 chance to spawn either a husk, a spider or a zombie)
Ranged and slow ranged (1⁄3 chance to spawn either a bogged, a skeleton or a stray)
Small melee (1⁄4 chance to spawn either a baby zombie, a cave spider, a silverfish or a slime)
Not all trial chambers will spawn with all four categories. However, the breeze category is guaranteed to spawn in all trial chambers.

Additional mobs

Chickens can also spawn in trial chambers through two methods:

In Java Edition, baby zombies and baby husks have a 5% chance to spawn a chicken jockey, which contains a chicken.
Dispensers can also contain eggs, which has a chance to spawn chickens.

== Trivia ==
The idea of trial chambers evolved from CornerHard pitching in a meeting to revisit the monster room.
The design of trial chambers was inspired by the architecture of Mesoamerican civilizations, whose geometric designs fit well into Minecraft. The design combines the aesthetic of ancient civilizations with a futuristic feel.
According to the article for the snapshot that initially introduced trial chambers, they are meant to be a mid-game combat challenge. However, the armor and weapons used by mobs in trial chambers, particularly during ominous trials, are more suited as an endgame challenge.
A scrapped chamber design appears in two YouTube videos showcasing the trial chambers and the trial spawners. It includes a trial spawner, two chests, and two redstone lamps with levers on top.
Brandon Pearce thinks that whoever created trial chambers in-universe seem to be "pretty tinkery".
There are around 4,500 copper blocks in an average trial chamber. If these were all mined and converted to copper ingots, this would be approximately 40,000 copper ingots.
Trial chambers can be used to obtain copper bulbs without entering the Nether.
