Chiseled sandstone is a decorative variant of sandstone, crafted from sandstone slabs. Its design depicts the face of a creeper.


== Obtaining ==


=== Breaking ===
Chiseled sandstone can be mined with any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, the block drops nothing.


=== Natural generation ===
Chiseled sandstone can generate in desert pyramids, warm ocean ruins and trial chambers.


=== Crafting ===


=== Stonecutting ===


== Usage ==
Chiseled sandstone is a decorative block, and has the face of a creeper engraved on it. See also Easter eggs#Mobs on chiseled blocks.
Unlike sand, chiseled sandstone is never affected by gravity.
