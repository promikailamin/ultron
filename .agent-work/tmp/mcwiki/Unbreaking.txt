Unbreaking is an enchantment that gives a chance for an item to avoid durability reduction when it is used, effectively increasing the item's durability.


== Obtaining ==
Unbreaking can be obtained from enchanting tables, drops from mobs that have spawned in a raid,‌[Bedrock Edition  only] books obtained by fishing, librarian villagers, and non-village structure loot.


== Usage ==
For tools and weapons, there is a 100%level+1 chance that using the item reduces durability, which means that for levels I, II, and III the item has a 50%, 66.6‾%, or 75% chance of not using any durability. On average, tools last 2, 3, or 4 times as long.

For armor, there is a 60%+40%level+1 chance a use reduces durability, meaning each durability hit against Unbreaking I, II, and III armor has a 20%, 26.6‾%, or 30% chance of being ignored. On average, armor lasts about 1.25, 1.36, or 1.43 times as long. Levels below 0 can lower the chance to reduce durability to less than 60 %.
When durability is reduced by multiple points, for example, when hooking a mob with fishing rods or getting blown up with TNT, Unbreaking is applied for each point of decision of actual reduction.
