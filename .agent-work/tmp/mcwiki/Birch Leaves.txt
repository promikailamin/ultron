Birch leaves are evergreen leaves that form the canopy of birch trees.


== Obtaining ==


=== Breaking ===
Hoes are the default tools for breaking leaves, but leaves can be obtained only with shears or tools enchanted with Silk Touch.

Without shears, birch leaves can drop 1 birch sapling, and 1—2 sticks. Fortune increases the chances.


=== Natural generation ===
Birch leaves occur naturally on birch trees throughout the Overworld. They can also generate as part of abandoned camps in birch forests, flower forests and forests.


=== Post-generation ===

Birch leaves generate as part of birch trees grown from birch saplings.


== Usage ==

Birch leaves that decay have the same drop chances as when broken by players, yielding birch saplings 5% (1⁄20) of the time, sticks 2% of the time, and otherwise nothing. Leaves that are burned do not yield saplings.

=== Redstone component ===

The state of a leaves block—including a player-placed block—changes after 1 game tick (half a redstone tick) when the distance to the nearest log or wood block changes, up to 6 blocks of leaves away. Observers facing away from the leaves detect this change and transmit a redstone signal in the same game tick, making leaves useful for redstone signal transmission.


=== Piston interactivity ===
Leaves are destroyed when pushed by pistons. They do not stick to sticky pistons, slime blocks, or honey blocks.


=== Composting ===
Birch leaves have a 30% chance of increasing the compost level in a composter by 1. A stack of leaves yields an average of 3.05 bone meal.


=== Color ===

Unlike most other leaves, the color of birch leaves does not change with the biome they are placed in, but always stays green. The texture is always colored with  #80a755.
In Bedrock Edition, this color is picked from a colormap which allows biome tinting, though the color is the same in every biome.


==== Snowy leaves ====

In any biome with a base temperature of below 0.15, birch leaves gradually fade to white once snowfall begins, giving them a frosted appearance for the storm's duration. During transition, the white color is blended with the regular color in eight steps. After snowfall ends, leaves gradually fade back to their normal coloration.
This does not affect leaves in biomes with a higher base temperature, even at altitudes that allow snowfall. The change is entirely invisible to the client if the graphics mode is set to Simple, though it still takes place, and leaves are the correct color phase for the time if the option is re-enabled.


=== Particles ===
Birch leaves produce falling leaves particles underneath the leaf block, where they slowly fall to the ground. Their color corresponds to the leaves block they fall from, which can change during snowfall.‌[BE  only]

== Trivia ==
Occasionally, leaves grow through other blocks, acting as though they are still connected to the tree. They may also completely replace blocks they try to grow into.
Some trees seem to occasionally drop saplings without being destroyed due to creation of leaf blocks not supported by logs.
