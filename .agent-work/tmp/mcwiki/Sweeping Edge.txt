Sweeping Edge is an enchantment applied to swords that increases sweep attack damage. This only affects the damage of the sweep attack against other surrounding entities, and does not increase damage against the entity that is attacked directly. Sweeping Edge is not required to do sweep attacks, as swords have the ability innately.​[until: JE Combat Tests]


== Obtaining ==
Sweeping Edge can be obtained from enchanting tables. Enchanted books with Sweeping Edge can be obtained by fishing, from monster room and ocean ruins chests, from librarian villagers, and as non-village structure loot.


== Usage ==
Sweeping Edge increases sweep attack damage by levellevel+1 of the sword base attack damage, meaning level I, II, and III deal 50%, 66.6‾%, or 75% of the sword's attack damage extra. Sweeping Edge is not required to do sweep attacks, as swords have the ability innately. Sweep attacks activate when a player standing on the ground attacks while not sprinting with an attack cooldown at 84.8% or above. The sweep attack damages surrounding entities that are within a 1 by 0.25 by 1 block area of the attacked entity, dealing 1HP and knocking them back. Sweep attacks and Sweeping Edge only affect sorrounding entities and not the attacked one.


== Damage ==
The formula for sweep attack damage is: 1+basedamage⋅levellevel+1+enchantmentdamage HP. Where base damage is the sword's damage before applying enchantments and enchantment damage is the damage added by Sharpness, Smite, and Bane of Arthropods for the target.
For example, an iron sword enchanted only with Sweeping Edge I deals 1+6⋅11+1=4HP damage to each surrounding mob hit with a sweep attack, while a diamond sword enchanted with Sharpness V and Sweeping Edge III would deal 1+7⋅33+1+3=9.25HP × 4.625 damage to surrounding mobs.
Higher levels than Sweeping Edge III (obtainable via the use of commands or third party editors) are processed following the formula above.
