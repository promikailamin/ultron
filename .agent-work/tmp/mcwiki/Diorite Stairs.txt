Diorite stairs are a decorative stairs variant of diorite that generate in snowy tundra villages and are used for building.


== Obtaining ==


=== Breaking ===
Diorite stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.


=== Natural generation ===
Diorite stairs generate as part of snowy tundra villages.
