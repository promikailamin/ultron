Efficiency is an enchantment applied to tools that increases mining efficiency.


== Obtaining ==
Tools of any material except stone or diamond can receive up to Efficiency V through an enchanting table. Stone and diamond tools can only receive up to level IV through the enchanting table, but can be given Efficiency V by combining 2 items with Efficiency IV in an anvil. Diamond tools with Efficiency V can also be found in end city, ancient city, and bastion remnant chests.
Enchanted books enchanted with a random level of Efficiency can be found in desert pyramid, stronghold, ocean ruins, ancient city, woodland mansion, pillager outpost, mineshaft, monster room, and jungle pyramid chests, and trial chambers vaults.
Enchanted books and tools with a random Efficiency level can be obtained through trading with villagers.


== Usage ==

When breaking a block with the correct tool, Efficiency adds 1+level2 mining_efficiency.
