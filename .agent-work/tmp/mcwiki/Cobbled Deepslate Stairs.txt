Cobbled deepslate stairs are a decorative stairs variant of cobbled deepslate that generate in ancient cities and are used for building.


== Obtaining ==


=== Breaking ===
Cobbled deepslate stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.


=== Natural generation ===
Cobbled deepslate stairs generate as part of ancient cities.
