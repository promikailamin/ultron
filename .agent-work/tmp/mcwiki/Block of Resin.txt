A block of resin, internally known as a resin block, is a block equivalent to nine resin clumps. It acts as a good temporary block being broken instantly without having any special properties, and can be used to craft creaking hearts.


== Obtaining ==


=== Breaking ===
Blocks of resin can be broken instantly, regardless of held items, or when under the Mining Fatigue effect. When broken, it drops itself.


=== Crafting ===


=== Natural generation ===
Blocks of resin can generate in pale garden abandoned camps.


== Usage ==
Because blocks of resin can be broken instantly without any tool, and do not have special interactions like slime blocks, honey blocks, and TNT, they can be used as a temporary building block that is easy to place and break.
