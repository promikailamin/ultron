Chiseled cinnabar is a decorative variant of cinnabar. Its design depicts a geometric spider.


== Obtaining ==


=== Breaking ===
Chiseled cinnabar drops as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.


=== Crafting ===


=== Stonecutting ===


== Usage ==
Chiseled cinnabar is used only for decoration. The block has a blocky spider engraved on its surfaces. See also Easter eggs#Mobs on chiseled blocks.
