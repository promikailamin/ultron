Chiseled sulfur is a decorative variant of sulfur. Its design depicts the face of a sulfur cube.


== Obtaining ==


=== Breaking ===
Chiseled sulfur drops as an item if mined by any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.


=== Crafting ===


=== Stonecutting ===


== Usage ==
Chiseled sulfur is used primarily for decoration. A chiseled sulfur block has the face of a sulfur cube engraved on it. See also Easter eggs#Mobs on chiseled blocks.
